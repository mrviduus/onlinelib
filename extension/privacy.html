TextStack — Save to read later — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

What this extension does

"TextStack — Save to read later" lets you clip the article you are reading, or send a document (PDF/EPUB) you have open, to your own private TextStack Read-later library.

What data is sent, and when

  • We send page content to TextStack only when you explicitly act — clicking "Clip this page", "Clip selection", "Send clip", "Send document", or the corresponding right-click menu items. Nothing is sent in the background or while you merely browse.
  • For an article clip we send the cleaned article HTML (extracted locally by Mozilla Readability), its title, detected author, the source page URL, and the detected language.
  • For a document we send the document file you have open.

Where it goes

Content is sent to your own TextStack account over HTTPS to https://textstack.app (or a developer-configured origin). Clips land on your private Read-later shelf and are visible only to you. The extension does not share data with any third party.

Authentication

The extension connects to your account using the OAuth 2.0 Device Authorization flow. Access and refresh tokens are stored locally in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never sent anywhere except to the TextStack API. Use "Disconnect" in Settings to remove them at any time.

Permissions

activeTab / scripting read the current page only when you trigger a clip; storage holds your connection tokens and settings; contextMenus adds the right-click items; alarms drives the one-time connection polling.
